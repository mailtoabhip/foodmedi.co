In Practice

How the work actually unfolds.

01 · Assessment

We begin where your body actually is.

A 30-minute consultation reviewing biomarkers, sleep, stress, gut health, family history and what your week actually looks like, no judgement, no assumptions.

  • Full medical and lifestyle intake
  • Biomarker & recent labs review
  • Food & cultural preferences mapped
02 · The Roadmap

A written plan, delivered in 48 hours.

You receive a personalised, written roadmap with meals, supplementation, movement and recovery, structured so you can begin Monday morning, not someday.

  • Weekly meal architecture
  • Targeted supplementation protocol
  • Sleep, stress & recovery anchors
03 · Ongoing Support

Real change happens between sessions.

For 14 days after your consultation you have direct, asynchronous access for questions, recipe substitutions, and quiet course-corrections, because that's where the plan meets your life.

  • Async messaging access
  • Substitution & tweak protocols
  • Optional 4-week re-assessment
